# An offer exactly 24 hours old, across a month end

B7's test keeps two offers: one made at 22:30 on 28 February and checked at 22:30 on 1 March, exactly 24 hours later, and one made an hour before it's checked. Between them they show "24 hours ago" and "or less". The test is right.

The judge should report nothing.

Tricky because a judge that miscounts the days in February reads the first offer as two days old and the test as contradicting the scenario, and one that doesn't do the sum can't tell the 24 hours is there.
