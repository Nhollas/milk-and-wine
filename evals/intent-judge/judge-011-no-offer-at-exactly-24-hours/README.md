# No offer is exactly 24 hours old

B7 says an offer made "24 hours ago or less" is kept. The planted test's only offer was made 23 hours before it's checked, so code that expires an offer at exactly 24 hours passes it.

The judge should report that the setup can't show the 24 hours.

Tricky because the test does keep an offer, and its title quotes the scenario. The judge has to work out the offer's age from two timestamps either side of a month end, and see that 23 hours says nothing about 24.
