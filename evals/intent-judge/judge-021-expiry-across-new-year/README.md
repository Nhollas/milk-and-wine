# An offer that expires in the next year

B4's test offers a place at 18:00 on 31 December 2026 and expects the message to say the offer expires at 18:00 on 1 January 2027: 24 hours on. The test is right.

The judge should report nothing.

Easy because the sum is one day. It's here to catch a judge that reads the change of year as a mistake.
