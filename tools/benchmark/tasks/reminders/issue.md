# Remind members about overdue loans

Status: open

Members forget to bring books back, and nobody tells them. A daily job should ask the organisation's Notifications API to remind each member with an overdue loan, once a day until the copy is returned.

Spec: [reminders](../../specs/reminders/spec.md). Every scenario in it is proposed: none is built yet.

The loans service and its catalogue client are already built, and their specs are under `specs/`.
